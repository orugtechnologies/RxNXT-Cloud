1. The Problem & Market Gap
Slide 02

The Critical Bottlenecks Crippling Indian Outpatient Clinics

Over 80% of India's 1.3M+ registered doctors operate standalone private OPD practices with acute administrative friction.

⏱️ Clinical Documentation

3 to 5 minutes wasted per patient typing into clunky legacy hospital software or illegibly scribbling on paper slips.

  • Causes doctor fatigue during peak 50+ patient footfall.
  • Leads to frequent dispensing mistakes by pharmacists.
  • Doctors abandon digital EMRs due to typing friction.

📉 Severe Patient Non-Adherence

Over 45% of acute patients forget dosage schedules or abandon antibiotic courses once initial fever drops.

  • Paper slips get misplaced or water-damaged.
  • Zero automated reminders for Morning/Night doses.
  • Chronic diabetes/BP patients miss regular refills.

⚠️ Regulatory Risk & Fraud

Spike in unqualified quacks writing illegal Schedule X/Narcotics without verified Medical Council credentials.

  • Government bodies (NMC) enforcing stricter audits.
  • No real-time license verification in conventional software.
  • Growing clinic liability in telemedicine prescribing.

2. The RxNXT Solution
Slide 03

An Ultra-Fast, Zero-Bloat Clinical Operating System

Engineered to deliver complete digital prescriptions in under 30 seconds with 100% automated post-consultation care.

⚡ Sub-30s Prescribing Engine

  • Additive Scoring Search: Exact alias match (+100 pts), doctor favorite (+50 pts), and clinic favorite (+20 pts).
  • 1-Click Quick Chips: Dosage, duration, and food timings selected in 1 tap (1-0-1, 5 days, After meals).
  • Protocol Packs: 1-click loading of entire treatment sets (e.g. "Viral Fever Protocol").
  • 1-Click Cloning: Re-prescribes previous regimens for returning chronic patients in 2 seconds.

💬 Direct Meta WhatsApp Automation

  • Zero Microservices: Direct enterprise integration with Meta WhatsApp Cloud API (Graph API v20.0).
  • Official PDF Delivery: Converts prescription into base64 and uploads directly to Meta Media endpoint.
  • Smart Slot Nudges: Automated daily 3-slot schedules (8 AM, 1 PM, 8:30 PM) based on food instructions.
  • Day-25 Refill Alerts: Proactive monthly reminder for chronic diabetic and hypertension care.

🛡️ Live Government NMC Verification

  • Direct NMC REST API: Live verification against National Medical Commission & 24+ State Medical Councils in ~0.4s.
  • Mandatory Gating: Workspace creation blocked until government credentials confirm legitimate doctor status.
  • Tamper-Proof Profiles: Official registered name, degrees (MBBS/MD), and council year auto-locked.

📄 Zero-Server-Cost PDF Engine

  • Client-Side Compilation: Prescriptions compile in the doctor's web browser using jsPDF.
  • Zero Server CPU Load: 5,000 doctors generating PDFs at the exact same second consumes 0 server compute.
  • Clinic Customization: Dynamic letterhead with clinic logo and doctor's verified digital signature.
