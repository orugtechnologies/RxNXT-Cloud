Strategic Roadmap Slide 09 / 10

Strategic Product Roadmap & Feature Expansions

Horizontal depth into Clinical Decision Support (CDSS), Integrated Billing, and Patient Self-Service.

🧠 1. AI Clinical Decision Support (CDSS)

Transforming prescription creation from transactional data entry into an intelligent clinical shield.

  • Drug-Drug Interaction (DDI) Engine: Warns doctor in real time if prescribed medicines have dangerous interactions (e.g., Warfarin + NSAIDs).
  • Pediatric & Geriatric Dose Sanity: Validates dosage based on patient age and weight.
  • Generic Cost-Saving Switcher: Suggests bioequivalent low-cost alternatives, saving patient costs.

💳 2. Integrated OPD Billing & Dynamic UPI

A unified cashier terminal combining consultation, procedure, and medicine billing.

  • Unified Invoice: Consultation fee + pharmacy bill combined into single checkout.
  • Dynamic UPI QR Codes: Printed on prescription and sent via WhatsApp with exact bill amount.
  • Daily Cash Register: Daily collection reports for clinic owners.

📲 3. Zero-Install Patient Web Portal

Empowering patients with lightweight mobile access without requiring an app store download.

  • OTP Authentication: Patient logs in securely with their mobile number.
  • Longitudinal Timeline: View historical prescription PDFs, past diagnoses & vitals trends.
  • Adherence Check-in: Patients tap "Taken" on their daily doses, providing compliance analytics to doctors.

☁️ 4. Permanent Cloud Archival & AWS S3

Enterprise long-term storage ensuring 10-year medicolegal audit readiness.

  • Direct S3 Upload: Automatic archival of encrypted prescription PDFs to AWS S3 / Supabase Storage.
  • Cryptographic Tamper-Proof Hash: SHA-256 hash validation for legal court admissibility.
  • Signed URLs: Time-limited, secure download links for patients and insurance providers.
