Doctors need more consultation time — not more typing.

Routine prescription documentation takes attention away from the patient and introduces severe medical risk.

FRICTION #1

Repetitive Data Entry

Doctors enter medicine names, strengths, dosages, frequencies, and durations from scratch 30 to 50 times every single day.

  • Typing takes 2 to 4 minutes per patient visit.
  • Creates cognitive fatigue and reduces patient engagement.
  • Forces doctors back to illegible handwritten slips.
FRICTION #2

Workflow Fragmentation

Clinical data sits scattered across paper slips, reception diaries, WhatsApp chats, and disconnected hospital software.

  • Front-desk queue is decoupled from doctor consultation chamber.
  • No instant access to past visit histories or chronic regimens.
  • Disjointed lab orders and day-care procedure billing.
FRICTION #3

Care Stops at Clinic Door

Once the patient leaves the clinic, clinical communication collapses completely, leaving treatment success to chance.

  • Pharmacists misread handwritten prescriptions (dispensing errors).
  • Over 60% of patients fail to follow dosage timings accurately.
  • Zero automated reminders for follow-ups or chronic refills.

RxNXT INNOVATION TECH
TECHNOLOGY DEEP DIVE #1 05

How RxNXT powers a complete prescription in under 20 seconds.

Three foundational software engineering innovations eliminate typing, modal switching, and latency.

ALGORITHM

Additive Scoring Search & Aliases

Sub-millisecond fuzzy search engine built with Fuse.js and custom clinical additive weighting.

  • Instant Alias Mapping: Typing PCM instantly resolves to Paracetamol (+100 pts short-circuit).
  • Doctor Frequency Weighting: Top prescribed drugs auto-boosted (+50 pts) to the top of suggestions.
  • Typo-Tolerant Heuristics: Matches phonetic mistakes (e.g., parctml) without query failure.
CLINICAL ENGINE

1-Click Protocol Packs

Pre-compiled syndromic multi-drug bundles that populate complete regimens with a single tap.

  • Standardized OPD Packs: "Viral Fever", "Acute URI", "GERD/Gastritis" pre-configured with 3-4 drugs.
  • Smart Defaults: Auto-injects clinical formulation, strength, timing (1-0-1), and food instructions (After Food).
  • Specialty Customization: Doctors save custom protocols in 1 click for future reuse.
REACTIVE ARCHITECTURE

Zero-Lag Client-Side Engine

Single-workspace reactive UI with real-time vector rendering.

  • No Modal Juggling: Center prescription dock updates the right-hand A4 preview synchronously in real-time.
  • Client-Side jsPDF Engine: Eliminates server roundtrips; vector PDF compiles in <100ms in browser memory.
  • Keyboard Accelerators: Complete flow executable via keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl + Enter to dispatch).
