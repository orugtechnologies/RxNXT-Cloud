1. The Healthcare Problem & Market Opportunity Market Gap

Over 80% of India's 1.3 Million registered doctors operate independent outpatient practices that are bogged down by manual paper slips or clumsy enterprise hospital software:

⏱️ 3–5 Min Documentation Loss

Doctors spend 4 minutes per patient handwriting paper or clicking through slow dropdowns. Leads to severe fatigue during peak 50+ OPD rush hours and dangerous pharmacy dispensing errors.

📉 45%+ Patient Non-Adherence

Paper prescriptions get ruined or lost within 48 hours. Patients forget meal instructions (Before vs. After Food), abandon antibiotic courses midway, and miss monthly chronic refills.

⚠️ Regulatory & Quackery Risk

Lack of real-time medical council license verification in current software exposes clinics to legal liability and allows unqualified staff to prescribe restricted Schedule X medications.

2. The RxNXT Solution & Clinical Moats Proprietary Technology

⚡ Fast Under 30-Second Prescriptions

  • Additive Scoring Search: Exact aliases (+100 pts), doctor favorites (+50 pts), and clinic formulary (+20 pts).
  • 1-Click Quick Chips: Dosage, duration, and meal timing selected in one tap (1-0-1, 5 days, After meals).

💬 Direct Meta WhatsApp Cloud API (v20.0)

  • Official Document Delivery: Prescriptions uploaded directly to Meta Media API and delivered as official PDF attachments.
  • Zero Intermediaries: Bypassed Twilio and web scrapers; direct enterprise Meta Graph API.
  • Smart Slot Dose Alerts: 3 automated daily schedules (8:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 8:30 PM IST).
  • Day-25 Chronic Refill Engine: Automated alerts for diabetic and hypertension patients.

🛡️ Live NMC Government License Verification

  • Direct National Medical Commission REST API: Live credentials query covering NMC and all 24+ Indian State Councils in ~0.4s.
  • Mandatory Gate: Workspace creation blocked until government records confirm legitimate practitioner status. Doctor's name and degrees are auto-locked.

📄 Zero-Server-CPU PDF Engine (jsPDF)

  • Client-Side Rendering: PDFs compile inside the doctor's web browser DOM.
  • Zero Compute Costs: 5,000 doctors generating PDFs at the exact same second consumes 0% server CPU, eliminating $50–$100/mo headless Chrome servers.
