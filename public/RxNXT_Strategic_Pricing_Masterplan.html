A Balanced, High-Margin Pricing Model Designed for Indian Healthcare Market Acceptance, High Conversion, and Lifetime Customer Retention.

📋 Executive Summary

In India, doctors are price-sensitive to upfront shocks, but demonstrate lifetime loyalty when pricing is transparent, fair, predictable, and directly tied to clear clinical ROI. The 3-Tier "Price-Lock" Model maximizes conversion, eliminates purchase friction, and builds predictable recurring cash flow.

🏆 The 3-Tier "Price-Lock" Model

🥇 Tier 1: BYOD SaaS Only

₹9,999 / year

Or ₹999/month. Ideal for single doctors with existing laptops/iPads. High conversion, 0 hardware cost.

🥈 Tier 2: Hardware Bundle

₹24,999 One-Time

Pre-configured 11" Android Tablet + Desktop Stand + 1st Year Subscription. Renews at ₹9,999/yr from Year 2.

🥉 Tier 3: Polyclinic Pack

₹18,000 / year

Covers up to 3 doctors (Effective ₹6,000/doctor/yr). Multi-doctor accounts & receptionist portal.

🧠 Strategic Psychology & Market Alignment

  • The "2-Patient" Rule: At ₹999/month, writing just 2 to 3 consultations (at ₹300-₹500 consultation fee) pays for the entire monthly software cost. The remaining 500 prescriptions are 100% pure profit!
  • No Forced Hardware: Doctors who have iPads start at ₹9,999/yr. Doctors wanting a dedicated device buy the ₹24,999 bundle. Zero friction.
  • Lifetime Price-Lock Guarantee: Early adopters lock in their annual subscription rate for life. Eliminates fear of sudden price hikes and drives massive doctor word-of-mouth referrals.
🔒 The Lifetime Price Lock Promise: "Whatever price you sign up for today is LOCKED FOR LIFE. Even as we add new AI features, your annual renewal price will never increase as long as your subscription stays active."
