🔬 Feature Deep Dive & Clinical Value

⚡ 1. Sub-30s Prescription Engine

Uses Fuse.js fuzzy matching with an additive scoring algorithm. Alias short-circuiting (+100 pts) turns "PCM" instantly into Paracetamol 650. 1-click quick chips (1-0-1, 5 days, After meals) cut writing time by 85%.

🕒 2. Integrated Queue System

Connects reception desk to doctor workspace in real time. Features live waiting timers ("Waiting 12m"), "NEXT UP" badges, and 1-click consultation launching.

⏰ 3. Smart Slot Adherence Model

Automates WhatsApp dispatches across 3 IST time windows: Morning (8:00 AM), Afternoon (1:30 PM), and Night (8:30 PM). Groups food rules ("Before breakfast" vs "After dinner").

🔄 4. Acute vs. Chronic Classifier

Acute courses (≤ 14 days) receive duration-limited Smart Slot nudges and stop automatically. Chronic courses (> 14 days) receive a 14-day morning briefing plus a Day 25 Monthly Refill Alert.

💡 Key Strategic Advantage: The Day 25 Refill Alert prompts chronic patients (BP, Diabetes, Thyroid) to schedule their monthly review, creating predictable monthly OPD revenue for the clinic!
