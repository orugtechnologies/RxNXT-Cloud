🔄 Step-by-Step Clinical & Administrative Workflows

📍 Workflow 1: Front-Desk Patient Onboarding & Queueing (Receptionist)

1. Patient arrives at clinic ➔ 2. Receptionist enters Name, Phone, Age, Gender ➔ 3. Checks DPDP Act Consent ➔ 4. Clicks "Register & Add to Queue" ➔ 5. Token generated (e.g. #14) ➔ 6. Form auto-resets in 0.5s ➔ 7. Patient appears live on Doctor's queue panel with waiting timer ("Waiting 2m").

📍 Workflow 2: Consultation & Sub-30-Second Prescription Generation (Doctor)

1. Doctor taps "Start Consultation" ➔ 2. Reviews patient history or clicks "Clone Prescription" for 1-click repeat ➔ 3. Types "PCM" (Fuse.js returns Paracetamol 650 instantly) ➔ 4. Taps 1-click pills (1-0-1, 5 days, After meals) ➔ 5. Clicks "Review & Send via WhatsApp" ➔ 6. PDF generated client-side with MCI signature and saved in Supabase DB in <50ms.

📍 Workflow 3: Automated Smart Slot Medication Adherence (Patient Care Engine)

Automated crons trigger WhatsApp dispatches across 3 IST time windows:
• 🌅 8:00 AM IST (Morning): Filters 1-0-0, 1-0-1, 1-1-1, OD ("Take 30 mins before breakfast").
• ☀️ 1:30 PM IST (Afternoon): Filters 0-1-0, 1-1-1 ("Take after lunch").
• 🌙 8:30 PM IST (Night): Filters 0-0-1, 1-0-1, 1-1-1, Bedtime ("Take after dinner/at bedtime").
• 🛡️ Superseding Rule: Updating a prescription on Wednesday cancels old pending Monday reminders (SUPERSEDED) so patient receives 1 clean updated message!

📍 Workflow 4: Chronic Care Management & Day 25 Monthly Refill Alert

1. Prescriptions >14 days (BP/Diabetes) receive daily 8 AM briefings for 14 days ➔ 2. On Day 25, automated WhatsApp alert prompts patient: "You have 5 days of regular meds remaining. Schedule your monthly checkup with Dr. Shanmukha Datta" ➔ 3. Patient books refill visit, driving recurring monthly clinic OPD revenue.

📍 Workflow 5: Pharmacist E-Prescription Verification & Dispensing Future Module

1. Patient presents WhatsApp PDF / QR code at pharmacy ➔ 2. Pharmacist scans QR via RxNXT Terminal ➔ 3. RxNXT verifies cryptographic signature & prevents Schedule X re-use ➔ 4. Pharmacist clicks "Dispense" ➔ 5. Clinic stock auto-deducts in real time.
