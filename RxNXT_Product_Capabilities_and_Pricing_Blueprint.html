1 Executive Summary & Platform Identity
What is RxNXT? RxNXT is a high-velocity Clinic Operating System (Clinic OS) that enables Indian doctors to write complete, legally compliant electronic prescriptions in under 30 seconds, while delivering instant digital prescriptions and structured daily medication schedules directly to patient WhatsApp without requiring patients to download any app.

Legacy electronic health record (EHR) platforms fail in Indian outpatient departments (OPDs) because they impose high typing friction on doctors seeing 30 to 80 patients per day. RxNXT solves this through Additive Search Scoring (learning doctor prescribing habits), 1-Click Curated Protocol Bundles, and Direct Meta WhatsApp Cloud API integration (delivering zero-cold-start messaging with attached official PDF prescriptions).

2 Complete Functional Module Breakdown (What RxNXT Actually Does)
🩺 Module 1: High-Speed Clinical Prescription Workspace (/doctor/prescription)
Users: Doctors, Specialists, Medical Officers

The core consultation interface optimized for minimum keystrokes and rapid patient turnover.

  • Additive Clinical Scoring Engine: Exact alias short-circuits (e.g. typing "PCM" brings Paracetamol to rank #1 instantly) + Doctor frequency weight (+50 pts) + Clinic preference weight (+20 pts).
  • Curated Clinical Protocol Bundles: 1-click execution of standard treatment regimens (Viral Fever, Hypertension, Type-2 Diabetes, Acute Gastritis) that prefill drugs, strengths, routes, frequencies, and durations.
  • Live Sticky A4 Preview Rail: Real-time reactive rendering of the final print-ready prescription alongside the input form. Eliminates guesswork and preview modal delays.
  • Regulatory Safety Enforcements: Automatic Schedule X / Narcotic drug blocking (in strict adherence with Indian Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2023) and mandatory dosage frequency/duration verification.
  • Instant Browser-Side PDF Engine (jsPDF): Compiles official prescription documents in <250ms with doctor registration numbers, council credentials, clinic letterhead, and tamper-evident QR codes. Consumes zero server compute.
📋 Module 2: Patient Registration, Queue & Triage Desk (/receptionist/dashboard)
Users: Receptionists, Triage Staff, Front Office

Manages front-desk patient intake, queue ordering, and preliminary clinical vital capture.

  • Fast Registration & Duplicate Prevention: Intelligent mobile number normalization (+91 sanitization) with real-time detection of existing patient records to eliminate fragmented clinical histories.
  • Live Token Queue Management: Automated token sequencing (e.g. #RX-001, #RX-002) with live status transitions (WAITING, IN_PROCEDURE, RETURNED_READY, COMPLETED).
  • Comprehensive Vitals Triage Logging: Captures Blood Pressure (systolic/diastolic), Pulse rate, Body Temperature, Weight, Height, and SpO2. Vitals automatically populate on the doctor's consultation desk.
💬 Module 3: Meta WhatsApp Cloud API Patient Communication
Users: Outpatients & Family Caregivers (Zero Mobile App Needed)

Direct server-to-server messaging via official Meta Graph API v20.0 with approved Utility templates.

  • Instant Super-Prescription Dispatch: Delivered immediately upon consultation completion with a structured dosage breakdown (Morning, Afternoon, Night) and a direct link to the official downloadable PDF.
  • Consolidated Daily Morning Briefing (8:00 AM IST): Groups all prescribed medications across Morning, Afternoon, and Night into a single morning alert. Eliminates spam while ensuring patients never miss afternoon or bedtime medications.
  • Automated Follow-Up Appointment Alerts: Sends a personalized reminder on the doctor's advised follow-up date to maximize OPD return consultation rates.
  • Chronic Care Day-25 Refill Alerts: Automatically reminds long-term care patients (courses >14 days) to schedule consultation refills 5 days before their current medication pack exhausts.
  • DPDP Act & Consent Architecture: Automatic unsubscribe handling; if a patient replies "STOP", the system immediately pauses outgoing reminders in compliance with Indian privacy laws.
💊 Module 4: In-House Clinic Pharmacy & Inventory (/pharmacist/*)
Users: In-House Dispensary, Clinic Pharmacists

Connects doctor prescribing directly to dispensary stock reduction and revenue reconciliation.

  • Batch & Expiry Management: Tracks batch numbers, expiry dates, rack locations, unit cost prices, and MRP selling prices per drug.
  • 1-Click Prescription Dispensing: Pharmacist dashboard displays pending prescriptions in real-time. Dispensing automatically decrements inventory count and creates an auditable DispenseLog.
  • Low-Stock & Expiry Early Warnings: Highlights critical inventory shortages and items nearing 60-day expiry to prevent clinical stock-outs.
🧾 Modules 5 & 6: Invoicing Desk & Clinic Staff Management (/admin/*)
Users: Clinic Owners, Practice Managers, Administrators

Financial reconciliation, multi-user role management, and clinic profile customization.

  • Multi-Line Consultation Billing: Consolidates doctor consultation fees, pharmacy line items, diagnostics, and discounts with payment tracking (CASH, UPI, CARD).
  • Direct Doctor Onboarding: Zero-friction doctor sign-up and clinic workspace provisioning without administrative registry delays, allowing doctors to self-register and start prescribing immediately.
  • Practice Letterhead & Profile Customization: Upload clinic logo, customize letterhead branding, upload doctor digital signatures, and format doctor qualifications (MBBS, MD) on prescriptions.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Strict clinic-level data tenancy isolating records between doctor, clinic_admin, receptionist, nurse, and pharmacist.

Why Daily Morning Reminders MUST Be a Separate Add-On / Opt-In:

Each WhatsApp utility message carries a direct cost of $\approx ₹0.13$. If an acute or chronic patient receives daily alerts for a 14-day course, that is 14 messages ($\approx ₹1.82$ per patient). A clinic seeing 1,000 patients a month could accumulate over ₹1,800 in direct messaging fees. Keeping this toggle OFF by default in the consultation modal protects software gross margins, while allowing clinics to purchase Patient Adherence Packs as a premium service.
